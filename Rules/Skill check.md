A Skill Check determines if a character succeeds at a task by comparing against a target difficulty number or wins a contest by exceeding an opposing skill check.
# Core Mechanic
A skill check uses 2 10-sided dice (2d10), and an appropriate skill
1. Roll 2d10 (two 10-sided dice).
2. If the lower die is less than the skill, set the lower die to the skill value.
3. Sum the 2 dice to get the final skill check result.
## Determining Success

- **Standard Task:** Compare the result to the Target Number. A result greater than or equal to the Target Number is a success.
- **Contest:** Compare the result to the opponent's result. The highest result wins.

# Examples

### Example A

_A Librarian is searching a library for 'Land rights to Cardonna' in their archive. Because the records have been lost the Target Number is 15. The Librarian is using their Search Skill of **7**. They roll a **3** and a **9**._

1. **Find the low die:** 3.
2. **Compare the Skill o the low die:** Is Skill (7) > Low Die (3)? **Yes.**
3. **Set low die becomes to the Skill value:** The 3 becomes a 7.
4. **Sum the dice for the result:** 7 + 9 = 16.  
    _(Without the swap, the total would have been only 12. Their Search skill has meant that they have succeeded and find 'Land rights to Cardonna')._

### Example B

_A Medic is trying to treat a soldier wounded in battle. The injury is serious and needs a Target Number 15 to save the soldier perfectly but a success of 11 means the soldier can be saved by amputating the leg. The medic has a Heal Skill of **4**. They roll a **5** and an **8**._

1. **Identify low die:** 5.
2. **Compare:** Is Skill (4) > Low Die (5)? **No.**
3. **Sum:** 5 + 8 = 13.
	_The Medic manages to save the soldiers life but he loses the leg_
