Vices are personality traits that negatively effect the character. On character creation draw a major arcana tarot card to get your vice. In addition, you can choose to roll for a reinforcement to the vice.

# Vice modifiers
+ Denial: the character does not see their vice as a bad thing
+ Obsession: The character must spend additional willpower to resist their vice
+ Compulsion: The character must regularly indulge their vice or exhaust willpower
+ Avoidant: The character takes great pains to hide their vice from others
